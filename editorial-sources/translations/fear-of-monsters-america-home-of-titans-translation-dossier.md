## Discourse map

- Thesis or objective: “Giant beasts” are not incidental entertainment. From colonial fossil interpretation to *King Kong*, the United States has repeatedly used them to establish claims about wilderness, nationhood, civilization, and human mastery over nature.
- Sequence of argument: mutual fear in Le Guin's planetary ecology; the Claverack remains as giant, sea monster, and American incognitum; the incognitum as a counter-myth to European accounts of American degeneracy; *King Kong* as a twentieth-century recurrence of possession, ethnography, technological conquest, and ambivalent mourning; the closing demand to reconsider a human-only measure of nature.
- Major turns and qualifications: human fear of beasts becomes beasts' fear of humans; religious and scientific interpretations disagree but share a colonial logic; possession of a monstrous fossil establishes both wilderness and sovereignty; Kong submits to the film's narrative while overturning it; humanity still “needs” beasts even after ceasing to fear actual animal herds.
- Pronoun or omitted-subject dependencies: “creator” near the close of section IV resolves to Kong as the figure through which the popular myth is created and destroyed, while Carl Denham and Cooper remain distinct fictional and real directors.
- Recurring wording and metaphors: fear; beast / giant beast / Titan; possession; homeland; wilderness; remnant of the Flood; camera / gun; jungle; anchoring and confirming identity.
- Ending function: revises the constitutional “We the People” into “We the Human,” then makes nature's response a question rather than confirmation of human sovereignty.

## Voice card

- Genre and register: historically minded cultural criticism with theoretical vocabulary and essayistic momentum.
- Sentence rhythm: long, accretive argument sentences alternate with short rhetorical questions and emphatic restatements.
- First-person and reader address: an inclusive “we” guides the historical reconstruction and returns in the ecological conclusion.
- Technical density: moderate to high; natural history, colonial ideology, ethnography, cinema, and environmental thought overlap without separate explanatory digressions.
- Irony, humor, and polemic: quotation marks around “calculated,” “possess,” and “homeland of elephants” carry skepticism; the Russia/Siberia comparison is deliberately dry; the final constitutional echo is earnest but critical.
- Target treatment: English preserves argumentative pressure without mapping Chinese topic-comment syntax mechanically. Japanese uses a contemporary critical-essay である register while varying endings and retaining the rhetorical questions.

## Exceptions and review evidence

- Protected-structure exception: the source presents Andrew Marvell's two quoted lines first in Chinese and then in English. The English edition retains the attested English once rather than printing each line twice; the Japanese edition retains the source's bilingual presentation. All other protected blocks remain in source order.
- Source discrepancy: the Chinese source reads `20世界初`, an evident malformed time expression. The public targets read naturally as `early twentieth century` and `20世紀初頭`; this editorial normalization is recorded here rather than exposed to readers as a bracketed cleaning mark.
- Source discrepancy: the constitutional quotation in the Chinese source prints `We the Peope`. The public targets restore the canonical phrase `We the People`; this editorial correction is recorded here rather than exposed as `[sic]` / `［原文ママ］`.
- Quotation routing: recoverable English quotations were checked against primary, publisher, archival, or official constitutional texts. English restores those originals; Japanese translates or checks them directly from the verified English. Wording not verified in a cited edition is rendered indirectly rather than placed inside quotation marks as reconstructed English or Japanese.
- Bibliography: all nine displayed records remain in their historical source form. No apparent spelling, pagination, or punctuation issue was silently normalized.
- Figure treatment: all four source asset URLs remain unchanged and in the same positions; alt text is translated without adding visual facts.
- Number warnings from the parallel-structure audit were inspected.
