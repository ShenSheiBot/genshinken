## Candidate versions and coverage decision

The official print and ePub editions establish the exact work identity beyond reasonable doubt: 東浩紀, `批評という病`, `ゲンロン4`, 2016-11-15. That is not sufficient under this batch rule. The necessary remaining verification is textual: the complete Japanese article (including its notes) must be inspectable so the Roof translation's first and last article sentences, the long note apparatus, and the editor-added formatting can be routed without inventing Japanese wording.

The Roof canonical's unusually extensive footnote apparatus raises the verification threshold rather than lowering it. In particular, `critic-disease-2` contains long nested material and repeated `【1】` / `【2】` blocks; without the Japanese issue text it is impossible to determine which portions are original authorial notes, Chinese editorial duplication, or another layer. Publishing a Japanese edition by “translating back” those passages would violate the source-routing rule.
