## Batch-01 segment-routing revalidation

The previous dossier incorrectly classified the entire essay as one direct Chinese-source segment. That treatment is rejected. The Roof article is a Chinese-authored compilation/manifesto containing protected quotations whose original languages include German, Japanese, French, English, and Chinese. Each quotation therefore requires its own source route while Roof continues to control selection, arrangement, ellipses, emphasis, images, signature, and Chinese-authored apparatus.

The rerouting recovered and aligned the German Benjamin passages from *Über den Begriff der Geschichte*, *Einbahnstraße*, and *Goethes Wahlverwandtschaften*, and recovered the exact English Žižek passage from the cited 1996 chapter. It also corrected the interview participant's name to **Bernard Accoyer** in research evidence rather than perpetuating the prior target's proper-name error. These findings are source evidence only; the Chinese canonical is not edited.

## Blocking source gaps

Two protected quotation routes still fail the source gate.

1. **Saitō Tamaki / 斎藤環.** The exact work and editions are verified: the 2000 太田出版 book was republished as the 2006 ちくま文庫 edition, and University of Minnesota Press issued the sanctioned English translation by J. Keith Vincent and Dawn Lawson. The selected passage can be located in the sanctioned English edition, but repeated searches of the publisher record, library records, preview endpoints, exact Japanese phrases, and in-volume search did not produce an inspectable Japanese source text for the quoted paragraph. A Japanese target must use the verified Japanese original when the target language is the source language; back-translating the Roof Chinese or the English translation into Japanese would manufacture wording and violate the routing rule.
2. **Europe 1 interview.** The interview identity and correct names are verified, and an English transcript corresponding to the Roof excerpt is available. A directly inspectable French transcript matching the quotation was not recovered. This is a second unresolved direct-source route; it is not the primary blocker because the Saitō gap already prevents a compliant bilingual target.

Search snippets, reviews paraphrasing Saitō, and third-party English mirrors were treated as discovery aids, not as a Japanese base text.

## Disposition

No English or Japanese target is created in this batch. The old targets remain deleted. Creating an English page while knowingly lacking the source required for the Japanese companion would leave the requested bilingual first translation in a partial state, and synthesizing the missing Japanese quotation would repeat the exact back-translation defect this batch is meant to remove.

Rendered-page acceptance is therefore not applicable: there is no compliant target route to open. Status: **skipped — source-unverified protected quotation; no first-translation completion claim**.
