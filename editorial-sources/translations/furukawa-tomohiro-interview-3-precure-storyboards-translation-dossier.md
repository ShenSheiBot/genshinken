## Discourse map

- Objective: explain how episode 8 of *Futari wa Precure* first made the concrete labor of episode direction and storyboarding visible to Furukawa.
- Movement: casual initial viewing → episode 8 as a sudden revelation → reverse-engineering the episode by redrawing its storyboards → contrast with Hosoda's deliberately series-breaking episodes → Igarashi's auteurial restraint in service of a TV series → discovery of omitted staging and storyboard control of time/rhythm → renewed commitment to Toei's long-running television craft → career shift from key animation toward direction.
- Core craft claim: direction is not mystical auteur inspiration; it is a sequence of concrete decisions about what to show, omit, pace, and control through storyboards.
- Ending function: *Precure* becomes not merely a favorite work but a direct cause in Furukawa's professional move toward directing.
