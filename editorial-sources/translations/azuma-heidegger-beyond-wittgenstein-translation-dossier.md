## Source classification and decision

The Roof canonical is a known Chinese translation by 养鸡场客卿, not a Chinese original. Its own opening note identifies it as an excerpted translation of Chapter 4, `存在論的、郵便的`, subsection 1, `論理的`, from Azuma Hiroki's book, with a translator-invented Chinese headline and bracketed supplements. The necessary paragraph base is therefore the Japanese 1998 Shinchōsha book edition. A Japanese target cannot be made by translating the Chinese relay back into Japanese, and an English target cannot be treated as direct from Japanese unless the complete Japanese span is available for routing.

Decision: `skipped-source-unverified`. Book identity, chapter, subsection, and broad printed-page boundary are verified, but the complete original-language text needed to verify every Roof-covered paragraph is not accessible from the examined authoritative/digitized sources. No target file was created.

## Discourse map (source-research only)

- Roof excerpt movement: Carnap's logical-positivist criticism of Heidegger → formalization of `nothing` and `Sein` → analytic/continental split → Wittgenstein's sayable/unsayable and logical form → Heidegger's attempt to think the limit/foundation → meta level versus object level → Dasein as the fold/short circuit between levels → Klein-bottle model → Gödel and Lacanian reformulation → existential analysis as a way to think the production of the limit/foundation.
- The final Roof claim presents Heidegger's method as a new account of transcendence or the movement of `meta, trans, über, hyper, plus`.
- This map is sufficient to guide future verification, not to authorize translation without the complete Japanese source.
