## EVA: JUNE interview

The quotation in `eva-subject-punishment-freedom/part-2` discusses the distinction between the intended ending and its methods of presentation, followed by the need to see other people's negative aspects. The matching publication lead is **Shōsetsu JUNE, August 1996, no.80**. A later collection, **残酷な天使のように 新世紀エヴァンゲリオンJUNE読本**, reprints the interview. See the [issue-by-issue description](https://evamark06.amebaownd.com/posts/51480519/) and the [Chinese transcription](https://evaresources.wordpress.com/staff/1997-june-anno/c-full-june-interview/).

These establish a useful retrieval target, not a verbatim Japanese match. The Japanese passage was not obtained. The separate Newtype interview cited in note15 supports the preceding claim and must not be substituted as the source of the JUNE dialogue. The existing passage has not been silently replaced with invented Japanese or an unrelated interview. Exact-original restoration remains open.

## Mori Minoru's Day of Resurrection

The verified English publication is Takayuki Tatsumi, translated by Christopher Bolton, **Mechademia 1 (2006), pp.87–90**, DOI **10.1353/mec.0.0032**. The volume's contents explicitly credit Bolton as translator. Searches by the exact English title, Japanese title variants and author name did not establish a separately published Japanese edition. This does not prove there was no Japanese manuscript or publication.

The existing Japanese edition remains identified as a translation, not an authenticated authorial original. No unrelated Japanese book or English publication has been substituted into a page promising access to the Japanese original. Replacement with an original-language access page awaits identification of that source.
