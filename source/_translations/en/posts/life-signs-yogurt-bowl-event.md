For this session of the Vital Signs Maintenance Tea Party:

**Topic:** A Guide to Making Delicious Meal-Replacement Yogurt Bowls\
**Time:** This Saturday (January 27) at 9 p.m.\
**Venue:** Dedicated event chat groups on QQ and WeChat.

The event will take place through group voice chat, text, and images. Come join us and share!

## About the Event

Meal-replacement yogurt bowls have been all the rage in fitness circles lately, billed as the perfect meal replacement—highly nutritious, low in calories, and tasty too. Tempted too?

Not so fast! Unless you read the ingredient list carefully and choose your ingredients well, that perfect meal replacement will turn into a calorie bomb in no time—with sugar content off the charts!

Want to know how to choose the right yogurt and ingredients? Join us in the tea party’s event chat groups on Saturday evening to hear fellow members share their tips. Let’s chat and learn together! (You’re welcome to share tips for making other snacks and recommend your favorites, too!)
