**Yare yare DA☆ZE—let’s see how the Hamon warriors and Stand users answer! (Strikes another JoJo pose.)**[^voices]

## 1. What do you do for work these days?

[fig] I’d rather become a “yangge STAR” than a Serie A football star.[^yangge]

![Giorno and Gold Experience strike a pose](attachments/roof-archive/cv2005395/02-giorno-gang-star.png)

*Image text—sidebar:* “What do you do for work these days?” *Subtitle:* “Giorno Giovanna would rather become a ‘Gang-Star.’”

## 2. Are you seeing anyone?

[fig] I spent my youth with DIO.

![Anime still of a character recalling his youth with DIO](attachments/roof-archive/cv2005395/03-youth-with-dio.png)

*Image text—sidebar:* “Are you seeing anyone?” *Subtitles:* “I spent my youth with DIO.”

[fig] You thought it was … but—kono DIO da!

![DIO makes his entrance](attachments/roof-archive/cv2005395/04-kono-dio-da.png)

*Image text:* “It’s me, DIO!”

## 3. You’re planning to get married soon, aren’t you?

[fig] One of the things I, Rohan Kishibe, love most is saying NO to people who think they’re stronger than me.

![Anime still of Rohan Kishibe refusing](attachments/roof-archive/cv2005395/05-rohan-says-no.png)

*Image text—sidebar:* “You’re planning to get married soon, aren’t you?” *Subtitle:* “But I refuse!”

## 4. How are your studies going? Where did you rank in the exams?

[fig] OH! NO!

![Anime still of a tower above the sea, with an exam-ranking question and subtitles rejecting effort](attachments/roof-archive/cv2005395/06-oh-no.png)

*Image text—sidebar:* “Where did you rank in the exams?” *Subtitles:* “The word I hate most is ‘effort.’ In second place: ‘Try your best.’” *On-screen countdown:* “31 days until the rings dissolve.”

[fig] Then atone by dressing up as the “Tequila Girl”!

![Anime still of Joseph in his “Tequila Girl” disguise, wearing a dress and carrying bottles](attachments/roof-archive/cv2005395/07-tequila-girl.png)

*Image text:* “I brought tequila! Can you let me in?”

## 5. A raise and a promotion this year, right?

[fig] There’s nothing I hate more than drawing attention to myself in front of other people (read: raises and promotions).

![Anime still of Yoshikage Kira talking about a quiet life](attachments/roof-archive/cv2005395/08-quiet-life.png)

*Image text—sidebar:* “A raise and a promotion this year, right?” *Subtitle:* “I, Yoshikage Kira, just want to live a quiet life.”

## 6. Still watching cartoons at your age?

[fig] So what if I watch cartoons? I can strike a JoJo pose, too.

![Anime still of a character striking a JoJo pose](attachments/roof-archive/cv2005395/09-jojo-pose.png)

*Image text—sidebar:* “Still watching cartoons at your age?” *Subtitle:* “Yes, I am!”

[^voices]: Translator’s note: The Chinese text imitates Japanese speech phonetically: Jotaro’s *yare yare daze* (“Good grief”) and, below, *kono DIO da* (“It’s me, DIO!”). [VIZ’s introduction to *Stardust Crusaders*](https://www.viz.com/blog/posts/stardust-crusaders) confirms “Good grief” as his English catchphrase.

[^yangge]: Translator’s note: “Yangge STAR” is a Chinese sound-joke on the Japanese *gyangu sutā* (“Gang-Star”); *yangge* is a Chinese folk-dance tradition. The joke also appears in [BB姬’s 2019 discussion of JoJo sound-play](https://www.ali213.net/news/html/2019-7/441727_3.html).
