The series has its missteps and goofy moments. Overall, though, judging by what it offers from Part 2 onward—the historical span it covers, the breadth of ideas it explains and discusses, and its production quality—it is one of the better introductions we currently know of to Japanese animation criticism, otaku culture studies, and otaku culture criticism in the form of videos edited from anime footage.

First, thanks to **the original creator, Pause and Select**, for bringing us this series and agreeing to its translation into Chinese.

Next, thanks to Lab on Roof’s volunteer translation community—群青七号楼—for completing the Chinese translation of the series over nearly six months.

Thanks to the team members who helped with translation, proofreading, subtitle timing, and uploading:
